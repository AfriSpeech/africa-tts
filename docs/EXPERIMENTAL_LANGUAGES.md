# Experimental languages: help us test

Africa TTS was trained on 92 African languages, but it reads **IPA**, not spelling. Any
language that [africa-g2p](https://github.com/AfriSpeech/africa-g2p) can write in IPA can be
fed to the model, and if its sounds are close to languages the model learned, it may already
speak it intelligibly, with no training at all. Bambara is close to Jula, Dagbani to Dagaare,
Abron to Sehwi, Tafi to Ewe.

We do not know how well this works, language by language. **Native speakers can tell us.**
Below are the 657 African languages that have an africa-g2p IPA table but were not trained on.

## How to test

```bash
pip install "africa-tts @ git+https://github.com/AfriSpeech/africa-tts"
africa-tts --list-experimental                       # tier, code, closest trained language, coverage
africa-tts "Your sentence in your language." --lang bam -o test.wav
africa-tts "Your sentence in your language." --lang bam --model africa-tts -o test.wav
africa-tts --ipa-only "Your sentence" --lang bam     # the IPA the model reads
```

The default voice is the closest trained language's; pass `--voice me.wav` (3-10 s of clean
speech in your language) to hear it in a voice of your language, which often helps a lot.

Then [open an experimental-language report](../../issues/new?template=experimental-language.yml):
the language, a few sentences, and a rating (can a native speaker understand it? does it sound
like the language, or like a neighbour?). If the IPA itself is wrong, that is an africa-g2p table
issue; please say which letters.

Languages that test well, and that someone can find recordings for, are first in line for the
next training run.

## What the columns mean

- **Tier:** A = all its sounds were seen in training and its inventory is close to a trained
  language (most promising); B = at least 90% of its sounds seen; C = the rest.
- **Closest trained language:** most similar phoneme inventory, with a bonus for the same family
  and country; its default voice is used.
- **Similarity:** phoneme-inventory overlap (Jaccard) plus the family/country bonus.
- **IPA coverage:** share of the language's phonemes whose symbols occur in the training IPA.
  Symbols listed under "unseen" were never heard by the model and will be wrong.

The tables come from africa-g2p's rule files; many are drafts not yet checked by a speaker
(`g2p_confidence` in `africa_tts/languages_experimental.json`). Generated by
`recipes/africa/release/experimental_languages.py`.
